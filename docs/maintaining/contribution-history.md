# Contribution history and relicensing boundary

Albucore is published under the MIT License. Its Contributor License Agreement
provides additional copyright and patent permissions for covered contributions,
but it does not change MIT permissions already granted for public revisions.
The operational boundary and the version-specific CLA requirements are recorded
in [license provenance](license-provenance.md).

CLA Assistant exports and entity acceptance records contain personal or
contractual information and are intentionally kept in access-controlled private
storage. Public Git history cannot prove that every contributor has supplied
rights for a future outbound relicensing. Before proposing one, a maintainer
must identify the affected files and commits, compare them with the private
Version 1.0 acceptance records, and obtain any missing permissions. That review
does not alter the MIT permissions attached to already published copies.
